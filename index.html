Clip Strip

A photo booth in your pocket. Photo strips, filters, frames and live clips.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email rhodri.development@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

How it works

Choose how many shots to take, pick a filter for the whole set, and pose. Choose your best four, drop them into a frame, add stickers, text or drawing, then save or share. Switch to Live and the strip becomes a looping video of all four moments.

Privacy

Everything happens on your phone. No account, no uploads, no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
