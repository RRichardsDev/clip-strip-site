Clip Strip Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version

Clip Strip does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Camera

The app uses the camera only to take your photo booth pictures. Camera frames are processed on the device to show the live preview and apply the filter you choose. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Photos

When you tap "Save to Photos" the finished strip is written to your photo library. The app asks for add-only access and never reads your existing photos.

Location

If you tap the location pin in the caption box, the app reads your approximate location once and converts it to a place name on the device so you can use it as your caption. It is not stored and not sent anywhere. The app never reads your location otherwise.

Sharing to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

The Instagram and Facebook Story buttons hand the finished strip to those apps using their share hand-off; nothing is sent to us. Facebook Post and TikTok use those companies' sharing kits, with analytics and advertising identifier collection switched off in this app. Once the strip is in their app, their privacy policies apply.

Sharing

The Instagram and Share buttons hand the finished image to the app you choose using the standard iOS sharing system. What happens after that is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Accounts, analytics and tracking

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.
